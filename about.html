Professional Profile

Adv. Siddharth D. Agrawal is the Founder of Adv. Siddharth D. Agrawal & Associates, with experience in litigation, legal drafting, legal advisory and dispute resolution. His practice encompasses a broad range of civil, criminal, family, matrimonial, commercial, corporate and property-related matters, with an emphasis on strategic legal advice, thorough preparation and practical, result-oriented solutions.

Professional Experience & Approach

He has handled and advised on matters involving civil and criminal disputes, family and matrimonial disputes (including international disputes), commercial matters, arbitration, corporate and business matters, property and title issues, writ & constitutional petitions, and MCOCA matters. His approach combines legal knowledge with careful case assessment, strategic planning, and effective representation.
