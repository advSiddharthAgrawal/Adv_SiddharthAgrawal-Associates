Practice Area

14. Specialized & Corporate Advisory

We provide specialised legal and corporate advisory services to individuals, businesses, companies, and organisations across a range of complex and evolving areas of law. Our approach combines legal analysis, strategic advice, and practical solutions tailored to the specific requirements of each client and matter.

Key Services & Specializations

Restructuring & Insolvency

Restructuring involves reorganising the financial, operational, ownership, or management structure of a business. Insolvency concerns the legal processes and consequences that may arise when an individual or entity is unable to meet financial obligations in the manner recognised by applicable law.

Intellectual Property

Intellectual property refers to legally protected creations, innovations, brands, artistic works, designs, and other proprietary interests. Depending on the subject matter, protection may arise through trademarks, copyright, patents, designs, or other recognised legal rights.

Mergers & Acquisitions

A merger generally involves the combination or integration of business entities or operations, while an acquisition involves obtaining ownership, control, assets, or a business interest in another entity. Such transactions commonly involve due diligence, approvals, documentation, and regulatory considerations.

International Trade

International trade concerns commercial transactions and business arrangements involving parties, goods, services, payments, or obligations across national borders. Legal issues may involve contracts, customs, regulatory requirements, international commercial practices, and dispute resolution.

Cyber Law & Cybercrime

Cyber law deals with legal issues arising from digital technology, electronic records, online transactions, data, internet activity, and computer systems. Cybercrime concerns offences committed using, through, or against digital systems and networks.

Pro-Bono Legal Services

Pro-bono legal services are professional legal services provided without charge, or in appropriate circumstances at a substantially reduced cost, to support individuals or causes requiring access to legal assistance.
