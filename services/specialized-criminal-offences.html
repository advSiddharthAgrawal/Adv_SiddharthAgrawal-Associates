Specialized Criminal & Financial Offences

Overview

15. Specialized Criminal & Financial Offences

Strategic legal representation in complex investigations, prosecutions, and regulatory proceedings. We offer professional legal assistance at every stage — from investigation and pre-arrest proceedings to trial, appeal, and other connected proceedings.

Key Practice Areas & Regulatory Defence

POCSO MATTERS

Comprehensive legal representation in matters under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, including anticipatory and regular bail, trial, appeals, revisions, and other connected criminal proceedings.

MPID MATTERS

Legal assistance in proceedings under the Maharashtra Protection of Interests of Depositors Act, including criminal proceedings, attachment of properties, release or restoration claims, and related litigation.

ED & CBI MATTERS

Representation before investigative agencies in matters involving the Enforcement Directorate (ED) and Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI), including summons, investigation, arrest, bail, prosecution, and court proceedings.

ANTI-CORRUPTION MATTERS

Representation in cases relating to alleged bribery, corruption, trap proceedings, disproportionate assets, and other offences investigated by the Anti-Corruption Bureau and other competent authorities.

PMLA MATTERS

Specialized legal assistance under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002, including ED summons, investigation, arrest, bail, attachment of properties, adjudication, and appellate proceedings.

"Professional legal assistance at every stage — from investigation and pre-arrest proceedings to trial, appeal, and other connected proceedings."

Frequently Asked Questions

When summoned, you have constitutional rights including protection against self-incrimination, legal representation during inquiry/summons under applicable guidelines, anticipatory bail applications under Section 438 CrPC (or Section 482 BNSS), and writ petitions before the High Court or Supreme Court.

Provisional attachment orders can be challenged before the Adjudicating Authority, Appellate Tribunal (PMLA), or Designated MPID Special Courts through petitions for release, restoration, or unfreezing of bona fide assets.
