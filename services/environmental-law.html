Environmental Law

Overview

7. Environmental Law

Environmental Law governs the legal framework relating to the protection of the environment, prevention and control of pollution, conservation of natural resources, and regulation of activities that may affect ecological systems. It covers regulatory compliance, project approvals, environmental disputes, industrial obligations, waste management, conservation, and proceedings before environmental authorities, courts, and specialised forums.

Key Practice Areas & Regulatory Services

Environmental Compliance & Regulatory Matters

Legal issues concerning compliance with environmental laws, regulations, licences, consents, permissions, and conditions applicable to industries, businesses, infrastructure projects, and other regulated activities. These matters may involve regulatory authorities and environmental compliance requirements at different stages of an activity or project.

Pollution Control & Environmental Liability

Matters relating to air, water, soil, noise, and other forms of pollution, including allegations of environmental violations, regulatory action, liability for environmental damage, and obligations to prevent, control, or remedy pollution.

National Green Tribunal (NGT) Matters

Environmental disputes and proceedings involving questions of pollution, environmental damage, statutory compliance, regulatory permissions, compensation, restoration, and other matters falling within the jurisdiction of the National Green Tribunal.

Environmental Clearances & Project Approvals

Legal issues relating to environmental clearances, approvals, permissions, impact assessments, and regulatory conditions applicable to infrastructure, industrial, construction, mining, energy, and other projects that may have an environmental impact.

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

Matters concerning the legal and regulatory framework governing the assessment of potential environmental impacts of proposed projects, including compliance with applicable procedures, public participation requirements, approvals, and challenges relating to environmental clearances.

Waste Management & Disposal Regulations

Legal and regulatory issues concerning the generation, handling, storage, transportation, treatment, recycling, and disposal of waste, including municipal, industrial, hazardous, plastic, electronic, biomedical, and other regulated categories of waste.

Forest & Wildlife Law

Legal matters concerning the protection and use of forests, conservation of wildlife and biodiversity, protected areas, forest land, project-related permissions, and regulatory restrictions affecting activities with potential ecological or wildlife impacts.

Industrial & Corporate Environmental Compliance

Environmental obligations applicable to companies and industrial establishments, including regulatory compliance, operational permissions, environmental audits, risk management, reporting requirements, and legal consequences arising from non-compliance.

Environmental Litigation & Public Interest Matters

Legal proceedings concerning environmental protection, ecological damage, pollution, public health, conservation, and enforcement of statutory or constitutional environmental obligations, including appropriate proceedings before courts and specialised forums.

Environmental Notices, Penalties & Regulatory Action

Matters arising from inspection reports, show-cause notices, closure directions, penalties, prosecutions, or other actions initiated by environmental authorities for alleged violations of environmental laws or regulatory conditions.

Climate Change & Sustainability Issues

Legal and regulatory issues connected with climate-related risks, sustainability obligations, environmental responsibility, emissions-related concerns, and the evolving legal framework affecting businesses, projects, and public authorities.

Environmental Disputes & Remediation

Disputes involving environmental damage, contamination, restoration obligations, compensation, remediation measures, and responsibility for preventing or addressing harm to natural resources and affected communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

The NGT has jurisdiction over all civil cases involving substantial questions relating to the environment, statutory compliance under environmental enactments, pollution control, project clearances, and environmental compensation.

Industries must submit structured legal responses backed by technical compliance reports within the stipulated timeframe, and if necessary, challenge arbitrary closure or penalty directions before NGT or High Courts.
