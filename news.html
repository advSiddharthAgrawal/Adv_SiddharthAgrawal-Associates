July 20, 2026

Supreme Court Reaffirms Section 138 N.I. Act Proceedings Can Run Parallel to Arbitration

The Apex Court reiterated that an arbitration clause in a commercial contract does not bar criminal prosecution for cheque dishonour under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, as criminal liability and civil arbitration remedies arise from distinct causes of action.

High Courts Directed Not to Summarily Quash Cheque Dishonour Complaints at Pre-Trial Stage

In Renuka v. State of Maharashtra, the Supreme Court clarified that High Courts exercising Section 482 inherent powers should refrain from quashing Section 138 complaints when prima facie statutory ingredients are satisfied, emphasizing full trial adjudication.
